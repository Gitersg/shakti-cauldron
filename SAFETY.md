# Safety

Shakti Cauldron is a study desk and a notebook. It is not a laboratory manual and not an emergency service.

- Starter practicals use water, table salt, red cabbage, or written work only.
- Do not mix household bleach with acid, and do not mix bleach with ammonia.
- Do not heat flammable oils in a closed vessel.
- Do not treat any page in this project as a recipe for a drug, an explosive, or a concentrated poison. Those procedures are not included, and you should not add them for other people to follow.
- If you formulate a real product such as market phenyl, use a supervised method or a licensed formula, wear the protection that method requires, and follow the label law where you live.
- This software is provided as-is, without warranty. You are responsible for what you do at a bench.
